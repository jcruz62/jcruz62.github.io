About me.

I'm Jade, a Biostatistics MS student at UC San Diego. Building a model is only the first step. I focus on inference, understanding how uncertain the results are and whether they hold up.

My current work includes the statistical analysis for a pilot clinical trial with head and neck surgery patients, a study of GLP-1 use and mental health using national survey data, and a simulation study on measuring uncertainty. At Handshake, I evaluate AI models and identify where they fail.

I'm seeking data science and machine learning roles at tech, pharma, and biotech companies, especially in clinical trials and health AI.
