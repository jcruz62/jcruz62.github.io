Clinical Trials

Head & neck patient guide pilot RCT.

Statistical review for a pilot randomized controlled trial (RCT) of patient guides for head and neck cancer patients undergoing free flap surgery.

Statistics collaborator and co-author (Ho et al., manuscript in preparation).

The problem

Free flap reconstruction is a major surgery, and patients often go in anxious and unsure what to expect. This pilot trial tests whether written patient guides change how patients feel and how they understand their illness.

The data

Survey data collected before surgery, two weeks before surgery, and after surgery, including the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale (HADS), a modified Brief Illness Perception Questionnaire (B-IPQ), and Likert items. Patient-level data are not shared.

Methods

Patients were randomized to a control arm, a standard guide, or a compassionate guide, with randomization stratified by age (under 65 or 65 and older) and flap type (soft tissue or bone). The statistical review re-ran the manuscript's Kruskal-Wallis comparisons by arm, with medians and effect sizes.

Findings

Verification of the manuscript's reported values and tests is complete. Additional analyses are on hold until more patient data are collected.

FOR THE TECHNICAL READER

The repository contains analysis code only. Spreadsheets, manuscripts, and other files with patient-level records are blocked from version control.

Next steps

Add the planned analyses and confounder adjustment once enrollment grows.
